    No. 07Questions

    Before you book.

    Still unsure?

    Message us — a real person replies, usually within two hours.

    Ask on WhatsApp
    01Do you send an operator with the equipment?

    Yes. The Complete Film Package includes an operator, and operators are available with other setups, including the DJ console. Mention it in your quote and we’ll include it.

    02Do you work outside Mumbai?

    Yes. We’re based in Mumbai and work pan-India — our gear has been on location from Rajasthan palaces to the Himalayas.

    03How quickly will I hear back?

    Within 2 hours. Send your gear list, dates and city and we’ll reply with availability and pricing.

    04How is pricing worked out?

    It depends on the kit, the number of shoot days and the location. The quote form sends us all three, so the first reply can be a real price.

    05I’m not sure what I need.

    Tell us what you’re shooting — tick “suggest a kit for me” in the quote form, or message us on WhatsApp — and we’ll put the right setup together.
